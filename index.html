What's Inside

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want to actually understand their money — not just track it.

Bills & Account Tracking
Track every bill with automatic status updates — Active, Pending, Overdue, or Paid. Auto-advances due dates on recurring bills so you never have to think about it.
Credit Card Hub
Dedicated hub for all your cards. Utilization bars, statement payment flow, and a full view of which services charge through each card — including wallet chains like PayPal.
Wallet Tracking
Model real-world payment chains — Netflix bills PayPal, PayPal bills Chase. Stash follows the chain so your credit card hub shows true load including indirect charges.
Savings Goals
Set goals with target amounts and deadlines. Track contributions over time. See projected completion dates based on your current pace.
Budget Planning
Set monthly limits by spending category. Visual progress bars show where you stand. A badge on the nav warns you the moment any category goes over.
PDF Reports & Statements
Generate branded monthly statements and stats reports as PDFs — ready to print, save, or share. All generated locally, no upload required.
OCR Bill Scanning
Scan a photo or PDF of a bill and Stash extracts the name, amount, and due date automatically. Supports JPG, PNG, and PDF files.
Encrypted Backups
Back up your entire Stash database to an encrypted file using AES-256-GCM. Restore it on any Windows machine. Your password, your keys.
Dark Mode + 6 Accent Colors
Full dark and light mode with six accent color presets. Stash looks premium regardless of your preference — ocean, forest, sunset, rose, violet, or slate.
Scan from Phone
Snap a bill or receipt with your phone camera — no app to install. Stash generates a QR code, your phone browser handles the rest, and the photo lands straight in the OCR scanner.
Net Worth Tracking
Watch your net worth move over time across every account, loan, and card — one number that tells you if you're actually getting ahead.
Funding Chain Visualization
See exactly how a bill gets paid — Netflix charges PayPal, PayPal draws from Chase — mapped end to end instead of buried in account notes.
